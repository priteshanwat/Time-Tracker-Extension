# Privacy Policy — Time Tracker

_Last updated: October 25, 2026_

Time Tracker ("the extension") is a browser extension that helps you track the time you spend on tasks and export your own time logs. This policy explains what information the extension handles.

## Summary

**The extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.** Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What the extension stores

The extension saves the following information **only in your browser's local extension storage** (`chrome.storage.local`):

- Task titles, categories, statuses and optional notes that you type
- Start, pause and end times and the calculated durations of your sessions

This data is created by you, is used only to show your timer, daily summary and time logs, and to generate the CSV and Excel files that you choose to download.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send any data to the developer or to any server.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or cookies.
- It does not read your browsing history, open tabs, web pages or any other websites.
- It does not use remote code, and it makes no network requests.
- It does not sync your data between devices.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | To save your tasks and time logs locally in your browser so they persist between sessions |

The extension requests no other permissions.

## Data control and deletion

You are in full control of your data. You can delete individual logs or clear a day's logs from within the extension. Removing the extension from your browser deletes all of its stored data. Files you download (CSV or Excel) are saved on your computer and are not accessible to the developer.

## Children's privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
